The Two Pillars of AMASAMYA

AMASAMYA bridges the gap between digital accessibility compliance and human career empowerment under one unified architecture.

1. Accessibility Software & Audit Suite

Architected & Engineered by Akhilesh Malani

Automated and manual testing tools built to be operated by screen reader users, not just for them. Evaluates digital interfaces against global WCAG 2.2 Level AA/AAA and Indian national standards.

  • Android App (Google Play): Live on Google Play Store (`org.amasamya.accessibility`). Features TalkBack Focus Trail visualizer, 48dp touch target inspector, and offline screen reader reading time analysis.
  • 24 Audit Engines: Colour Contrast (4.5:1), Focus Visibility, Target Size, Landmarks, Forms, ARIA Validation, and Accessible Authentication.
  • Indian Standards Mapping: Every finding carries its Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and BIS IS 17802 reference alongside the WCAG success criterion.
  • Multi-Platform Extensions: Chrome & Edge extension, Firefox add-on (v5.3.0), and Web Audit Portal.
  • VPAT 2.5 INT Reporting: 1-click generation of official VPAT 2.5 ACR reports in Executive PDF, Excel CSV, HTML, and Markdown format.

2. AMASAMYA Academy

School of English & Communication Co-Led by L. Subramani

An inclusive, screen-reader native learning ecosystem where blind learners and mainstream professionals build corporate English mastery and accessibility engineering skills side by side.

  • School of English & Communication: Curated by L. Subramani, author of Lights Out (Ebury Press). Foundations, Corporate Writing, Executive Speech, and Journalism.
  • School of Accessibility Engineering: Led by Akhilesh Malani. Hands-on WCAG 2.2 auditing, Jetpack Compose UI accessibility, and GIGW 3.0 mastery.
  • Audio-to-Text Writing Studio: Accessible drafting canvas with live statistics and the Subramani copyediting checklist.
  • Blind-Educator Authoring Tool: In-browser audio recording and non-visual quiz builder for educators with disabilities.
